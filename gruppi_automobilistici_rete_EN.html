Open points
Nissan's reduction in Renault from 15% to 10% remains a declared but unexecuted intention. The Kuwait Investment Authority stake in Mercedes and the Quandt split in BMW have no single primary document. Geely's stake in Aston Martin comes from the press, not a filing. Volkswagen's stake in Traton (about 90%) and Mitsubishi Corporation's stake in Mitsubishi Motors are not confirmed on a primary source in either research. Lower Saxony's capital stake in Volkswagen (about 11.8%) remains an estimate not precisely re-checked. The capital/vote split of Exor and of Piero Ferrari in Ferrari (respectively about 21.3%/32.3% and 10.7%/16.2%) comes from an industry analysis, not an Exor or Ferrari filing. The split of voting rights of AB Industrivarden and of the Wallenberg sphere in AB Volvo was not confirmed on a primary source. The closing status of the Iveco Group acquisition by Tata Motors is ambiguous among the sources consulted as of July 2026: some indicate it as already concluded, most indicate it still expected in the third quarter of 2026. Tata Motors' total control of Jaguar Land Rover since 2008 is established industry knowledge, not re-verified against a filing in this session.
